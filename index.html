Yin Zhang

I’m a first-year master’s student in Computer Engineering at NYU Tandon. I’m interested in how language models develop behavioral tendencies and how training reshapes these behaviors. I’m particularly drawn to the moments of vulnerability that emerge in these systems. I approach these questions with a humanistic outlook.

Lately, I’ve been thinking about persona emergence and subliminal learning.

I’m actively seeking summer internships and research collaborations. I am also looking for PhD opportunities starting in Fall 2028.

Research Interests

  • Mechanistic Interpretability: Understanding how internal representations, features and circuits give rise to model behavior and developing trustworthy causal interventions for model safety and reasoning.
  • Trustworthy AI: Advancing reasoning faithfulness and robust safety mechanisms in LLMs.
  • Multimodal Safety & Efficiency: Improving VLM training efficiency and cross-modal safety alignment through information-theoretic reinforcement learning.

Beyond that, I’m enthusiastic about Computational Art, Generative Art, and Digital / Visual Art. I’m especially interested in interdisciplinary projects that bring together art, technology and humanity.
